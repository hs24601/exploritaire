## Integration

The later [painted den orbit pass](C:/dev/Exploritaire/docs/art/watercolor-den-orbit-v1.md) supersedes the single-view den experiment described here. The front source is retained, with matching side, quarter and closed rear paintings selected relative to its fixed north-facing entrance.

The shared scenery selector updates map, card and den landscape artwork. Neighbor-specific mountain pieces and seam sources pass through the same selector, rather than placing pixel filenames in a painted asset directory. Connected prints and floors use smooth sampling and tile-union clipping, preserving the den notch and concealed cells.

Following the user's corrections about voxelization and camera-following rotation, Paint displays the original full-resolution den PNG as a fixed north-facing plane. Its active volume recipe was removed: smooth sampling alone did not remove the carved geometry's visible steps. Its ground anchor and world heading stay fixed through orbit; the image does not turn toward the camera. Lighting, silhouette shadow, canopy and actor occlusion share that heading. OTT artwork/roof, actor placement and resting-actor heading/occlusion behavior are retained. Pixel keeps its original north-facing carved cave. No new art was generated for these corrections. A single-view painting foreshortens during side views; it does not supply a separately authored back view.

Mountains and seam ridges show the original camera-facing paintings with fixed contained ground anchors, using the shared cutout path approved for woods/bushes. Main peak paint is reused at the established end/corner scales. Rounded source reconstruction distorted the mountain brushwork into swirled cones during enlarged visual inspection, so it is not selected. Lighting, silhouette shadow and actor-occlusion integration remain shared. No gameplay rules change.
