# Fast Swipes: discrete alternating exposures

The approved revision replaces v4's continuously translating phantom paws with a simulated printed lenticular sequence. Six independently ImageGenerated body drawings alternate left gather, left strike, left follow-through, right gather, right strike and right follow-through. The wrist, silhouette, horizontal endpoint and foreshortening change in each drawing. Mochi's face, torso, camera and canvas remain registered. Three separately generated six-cell phantom atlases provide far/middle/near echoes with staggered exposures and different horizontal placements. They do not duplicate a single paw stamp.

Actual supplied references were Mochi's v4 ready identity/registration master, Scratch v3 for the lighter watercolor swatches/soft wet brushwork, and approved Hero v6 for the established painting baseline. All nine built-in ImageGen prompts, generated paths, reference roles, original dimensions, alpha counts and SHA-256 digests live in `public/assets/sources/abilities/fast-swipes/body-and-phantom-atlases/manifest.json`. Masters and matching runtime originals preserve generated alpha byte for byte. The six body originals are deterministically downsampled/packed into a 3×2 atlas of 768×512 cells by `tools/prepare-fast-swipes-v5.cjs`; the generated phantom atlases already contain six square cells. No color-keying, recoloring or procedural replacement artwork is used.

`src/proto/fastSwipesLenticular.ts` shares pose order, plane geometry, stagger phases and discrete X jumps between the DOM fallback and GLSL. Signed lateral input selects `floor(positiveModulo(x*12+phase,6))`, with a .006 center deadband. Each center-to-edge sweep reveals two alternating-arm cycles. Reversing the angle reverses the exposure sequence. No elapsed-time uniform, motion accumulator, sinusoidal paw translation or position tween exists. Background optics still respond to tilt. Lens slivers exchange nearby printed exposures only in a narrow region around their gates (roughly 16% of one exposure band); there is no full-card pose dissolve.

The shader samples five textures for Fast Swipes: body atlas, background and three phantom atlases. It retains the uniform full-art coating and global strength/pitch/toggle. Bite's four jaw frames and its existing angular calibration remain unchanged. Context loss/loading/disabled WebGL uses the same discrete DOM poses. Reduced motion and low/off FX show the first body pose and hide all phantoms. Labels, rarity, stationary hitboxes and gameplay remain separate from art selection.

Validation is recorded by `tools/fast-swipes-exposures-check.cjs`: actual pointer sweeps in both directions and negative angles, repeated cycles, identical plane placement within an exposure, staggered plane poses, idle and vertical-angle stability, context-loss fallback/recovery, global toggle, phone rendering, reduced motion and injected orientation events. Screenshots and results are saved in `output/swipes-v5-*`. Injected orientation is not physical-device verification.
