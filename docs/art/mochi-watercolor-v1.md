# Mochi watercolor sprites v1

Built-in ImageGen, 2026-10-13. Authorized replacement for Mochi in Paint OTT, map immersion and battle. Pixel originals, card artwork and den sleeping/cuddle artwork remain preserved. New Paint map travel/standing uses the existing Mochi quadruped definition through the shared TableActorSprite renderer; no gameplay or hit-area change.

Runtime atlases:
- `public/assets/actors/battle/watercolor-v1/mochi-directions-low-watercolor-v1.png`
- `public/assets/actors/battle/watercolor-v1/mochi-directions-high-watercolor-v1.png`

Sources: `public/assets/actors/sources/mochi-watercolor-v1/{low,high}-source.png`. Packing measurements: `mochi-watercolor-v1-packing.json`. Run `node tools/prepare-mochi-watercolor.cjs public/assets/actors/sources/mochi-watercolor-v1/low-source.png public/assets/actors/sources/mochi-watercolor-v1/high-source.png`. Offline connected-component isolation excludes adjacent bodies before uniform packing into the existing Mochi rectangles. Source alpha above 32 is retained, including partial pigment edges; faint haze below that threshold is discarded.

Validation: 17 atlas crops preserve silhouettes and exclude neighbors; rendered gallery at `artifacts/mochi-watercolor-v1/rig-gallery.png` covers all eight headings at low/elevated/OTT. The overhead correction reduces the upward-looking face; a small nose/eye sliver remains in the authored print. Sleeping/cuddle and holocard conversions are outside this standing/directional pass.

## Prompts

### Low
Use case: stylized-concept. Production transparent game sprite sheet for Mochi, a cute chunky long-haired tortoiseshell CAT. Input image 1 is the exact identity, anatomy, markings, poses and direction layout reference; image 2 is watercolor brushwork reference only, do not copy dog anatomy or flame. Repaint complete coherent cats preserving large rounded head, large expressive amber-green eyes, pointed tufted cat ears, compact fluffy torso, short legs, rounded dark paws and substantial furry plume tail. Preserve asymmetric charcoal/copper/tawny coat patch placement and the different left/right sides. Broad irregular wet brush dabs, few large merged pigment pools, rich charcoal and warm copper/tawny, soft wet-on-wet transitions, diluted lost edges and selective pooled dark boundaries. Suggest fur by masses, deliberately lose tiny hair detail. Sparse broken charcoal ink accents for eyes/nose/mouth and major anatomy only. NO grain/stippling, tiny fur strokes, serrated shards, dense outlines, polished digital shading, white socks, dog features or flame tail. True transparent background and generous transparent gutters, no ground shadow, scenery, labels, grid, paper, checkerboard, signature. All complete silhouettes separated, no overlaps or clipping.
Exactly EIGHT complete standing cats in a regular FOUR column TWO row sheet, equal cells. Row1: front facing viewer; left front-quarter nose left; left profile nose left; left rear-quarter facing away left. Row2: rear facing away; right rear-quarter facing away right; right profile nose right; right front-quarter nose right. Low near ground eye-level camera. Match identity reference directions and neutral standing stance exactly.
