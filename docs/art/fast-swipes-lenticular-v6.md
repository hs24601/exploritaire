# Fast Swipes: restrained right-paw slap

The v6 ImageGen pass replaces v5's alternating arms and three staggered phantom planes with four drawings of one short cat slap. Only Mochi's anatomical right foreleg, attached on the viewer-left, changes pose. Her opposite foreleg hangs down and stays generally fixed. The four exposures are rest, lift, slap and follow-through, with retracted claws and two tiny broken speed marks instead of surrounding swooshes or magical effects. Earlier versions remain archived.

Actual supplied references: v5 Mochi identity/registration master, Scratch v3 for light diluted watercolor and soft wet brushwork, and approved Hero v6 as style baseline. After generating the new down-paw rest master, each action edit used that actual image to preserve the stationary left paw, face, body, tail and camera. Exact built-in ImageGen prompts/reference roles, generated paths, source/runtime byte identity, alpha counts and SHA-256 digests are recorded in `public/assets/sources/ui/holocards/mochi-fast-swipes/v6/manifest.json`. Runtime originals live in the matching `public/assets/runtime/ui/holocards/mochi-fast-swipes/v6/` folder. Original generated alpha is retained without keying or recoloring; there is no packed or procedural replacement subject.

The shared DOM/GLSL sequence uses just three signed gates, -0.45, 0.20 and 0.65, across the full -1…1 lateral range. There is no modulo, repeat cycle, phantom sampler, limb translation or tween. Thus a full sweep makes three pose exchanges, compared with 24 body exchanges plus staggered phantom exchanges in v5. Face-on selects lift. Reversing tilt retraces the same drawings; holding an angle freezes the action. A .024-wide lens-sliver transition exchanges neighboring exposures at a boundary; the shared full-art optical coating and its strength/pitch/toggle remain. Bite's jaw sequence is unchanged.

The DOM uses the four original images, while WebGL samples their four bounded textures plus the existing background. Context loss/loading/disabled WebGL retains discrete DOM selection. Reduced motion and low/off FX use the first rest painting. No gameplay, rarity, cost, recruitment or scene-start rules change.

`tools/fast-swipes-slap-check.cjs` checks actual pointer sweeps/reversal with exactly three exchanges, idle and vertical stability, absence of phantom planes, GPU context recovery, DOM fallback, phone rendering, injected gyro, global toggle, reduced motion and manual recruitment. It also audits original alpha/digests and produces light/dark/checkerboard review sheets. Results/screenshots are under `output/swipes-v6-*`. Injected orientation is not a physical-device test.
