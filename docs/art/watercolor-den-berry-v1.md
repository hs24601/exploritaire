# Watercolor den and berry patch v1

Berry artwork is superseded by [watercolor-berries-v2.md](./watercolor-berries-v2.md); den artwork and rendering are superseded by [watercolor-den-mountains-v2.md](./watercolor-den-mountains-v2.md). The ground asset below remains current. Original v1 sources and prompts are preserved here.

Eric requested the same direct painted-asset treatment as the approved trees/woods on 2026-10-12. Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool; original Pixel files remain intact.

Runtime assets live in `public/assets/biomes/watercolor-v1/`:

- `hero-den-sprite-v1.png`: front slate-boulder cave cutout.
- `hero-den-overhead-v1.png`: overhead roof and hollow, authored opening toward the bottom and rotated 180° for the game's north-facing mouth.
- `berry-patch-sprite-v1.png`: separate low berry bushes with visible gaps.
- `berry-patch-overhead-v1.png`: matching overhead crowns.
- `woodland-ground-v1.png`: shared full-bleed painted earth beneath these two places, flush with the table.

The shared `sceneryArt.ts` selects Paint counterparts for the map, roof and inspection cards. Cutouts have no volume recipe: preserve generated alpha, source colors and smooth sampling, billboard toward the orbital camera, reserve every heading's ground footprint up front, and retain shared silhouette shadows and live actor peek masks. Existing den actor poses, Pixel solids, discovery, travel and resource rules are unchanged. No other scenery is repainted.

Validation screenshots and measurements are in `artifacts/painted-den-berry-v1/`. The production build, 20 focused unit tests, and isolated mouse/touch checks passed, including full orbit, live style switching, actor peek and the ten-card deal yielding two mana. See `validation.md` in that artifact directory for the actual checks.

## Den front

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent watercolor cave-den game sprite for Exploritaire. Primary request: a single complete squat rocky animal den, strict orthographic front elevation, opening centered facing the viewer, width about 1.65 times height. Large uneven slate boulders form a low arch around a deep dark hollow, a small warm brown earthen threshold inside the entrance. Style: match the approved painted pine trees' loose hand-dabbed watercolor: broad irregular wet brush dabs and large pigment pools, soft wet-on-wet blooms, selectively lost detail, varied feathered edges, sparse broken charcoal/indigo brush accents. Palette: slate blue gray, muted lavender-gray and cool charcoal rocks with warm umber earth, no uniform pale wash. Simple broad boulder masses, only a few broken crevice accents, no detailed gravel or rock microtexture. Composition: entire den including both side walls and uneven dome roof in a compact landscape silhouette with generous transparent gutters and ground contact feet aligned. Hollow visibly open and very dark, not a painted door. Neutral diffuse light, no cast ground shadow. Genuinely transparent background. No animals, no grass, no extra trees, no mountains, no surrounding landscape, no backdrop, no paper rectangle, no labels or signature. Avoid pixel art, voxels, square steps, polygon faceting, digital rendering or dense sharp outlines. Save as a project-ready transparent PNG cutout.
