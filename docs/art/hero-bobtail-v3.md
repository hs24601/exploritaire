# Independent natural bobtail layers

Built-in ImageGen edits, 2026-10-13. Every current natural-bobtail sprite is split into body and natural-tail layers: standing low/high, sitting low/high, all four walking phases low/high, flinch, down, sleeping and sleeping overhead. The former baked versions remain historical references.

Runtime atlas pairs live in `public/assets/runtime/characters/hero/rig/watercolor/v3/`; sleeping pairs live in `public/assets/runtime/characters/hero/sleeping/watercolor/v1/`. Each `*-body-v3.png` has a registered `*-bobtail-layer-v3.png`. Prompts and generated paths are in `hero-bobtail-v3-generation.json`; original generated PNGs are retained in `public/assets/sources/characters/bobtail-v3/`. `tools/prepare-hero-tail-layers.cjs` records the authored extraction regions and preserves every original body pixel outside those regions. ImageGen reconstructs the rump under the separated natural nub. No new anatomy is assembled from independently generated body parts.

`getHeroBobtail`, `setHeroBobtail` and `useHeroBobtail` share a session setting, **on by default**. Game settings exposes **Hero bobtail**, independently of **Hero flame tail**. `DirectionalSpriteInstance.setBobtailVisible(boolean | undefined)` supplies a per-instance override; undefined returns to the shared setting. `instance.bobtail` follows the live hind joint, active pose, walking phase, camera tier and source UV registration. It shares environment light, tint, hit-flash uniforms and actor opacity. Its geometry/material disposal is explicit. Visibility changes do not mutate the body, gait, gameplay hit regions or flame socket.

Resting prints, cave interiors and resting quest decorations composite the matching tail layer with the same placement as the body. Resting bounds retain the original complete-source trim, independently of visibility. Target outlines include the independently visible tail. Historical Pixel fire/plume artwork contains no newly authored natural bobtail and stays preserved; portraits, quest illustrations and combined legacy Hero/Mochi cuddle artwork are not part of the natural-bobtail sprite rig.

Validation: focused rig tests cover five poses, three camera tiers and eight headings, override/default behavior, body invariance and opacity. `tools/hero-bobtail-layers-playwright.cjs` exercises settings and den visibility using mouse/touch at 1280x720 and 1912x914, checks settings layout and renders on/off pose galleries in `artifacts/hero-bobtail-v3/`. Production build and typecheck passed.
