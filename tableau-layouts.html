Tableau layout ideas

Static mockups of eight layouts that feed one or more foundations from several candidates, with the 3×3 grid and the 7-column tableau as baselines. The levers that keep a layout in the goldilocks zone are how many candidates are live (roughly 4 to 7) and how fast the board refills. Ranks are placeholders.
